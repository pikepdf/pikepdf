sRGB ICC profile (src/pikepdf/pdfa/data/sRGB.icc)
=================================================

The sRGB output profile that pikepdf.pdfa installs as the default PDF/A
output intent. It is the sRGB.icc profile from the icc-profiles-free
collection (as packaged by Debian). The profile's own copyright tag reads
"no copyright, use freely"; the collection distributes it under the zlib
license below.
