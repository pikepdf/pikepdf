LicenseRef-license-text-verbatim

This identifier marks files in `third-party-licenses/` that are verbatim copies
of another project's license text or license notice, reproduced so that pikepdf
binary wheels carry the attribution those licenses require.

These files are reference documents. They are not part of pikepdf's covered
software, they impose no terms on pikepdf, and pikepdf claims no copyright in
them. Each remains the work of its respective author, is reproduced without
modification, and may be copied and distributed verbatim under the terms the
document itself states.

`third-party-licenses/README.md` maps each vendored binary component in a
pikepdf wheel to the license text that applies to it.
